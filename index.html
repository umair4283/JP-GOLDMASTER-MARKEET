Core Strategies

Three Distinct Trading Architectures

Explore how each trading mechanism operates, its logic, parameters and risk controls.

Scalping Strategy

High-frequency micro execution aiming to capture small price movements over seconds or minutes.

  • EMA filter: fast 9 / slow 21
  • RSI momentum filter, period 14
  • VWAP anchor and ATR adaptive stop
  • Risk per trade 0.25% to 1.0%
  • Spread, slippage and news-pause filters

Hedging Strategy

Risk-management technique that offsets exposure with an opposite or correlated position.

  • Direct, partial and correlation hedge modes
  • Dynamic hedge ratio allocator (10%–100%)
  • Multi-pair drawdown protection
  • News event auto-pause

Martingale Strategy

Position-sizing recovery method that increases size after a loss.

  • Configurable multiplier (1.25x–2.0x)
  • Hard 5-level circuit breaker and emergency stop
  • High risk: prolonged losing streaks can cause large drawdowns or margin wipeout.
